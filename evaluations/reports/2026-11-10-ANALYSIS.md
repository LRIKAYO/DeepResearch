## Reading

- The largest cutoff covers **25% of the whole Project corpus**. Recall at that cutoff saturates for geometric reasons, not retrieval quality: report a smaller cutoff as the headline, or grow the corpus.
- Recall@1 is 0.900: about 4 answerable case(s) put the right passage second or later, even though every one of them is recovered by k=5.
- No case ranks a distractor above every relevant passage, so the failures are **tail contamination**, not wrong-answer-wins. That is the cheaper failure to fix: rerank or trim the window.
- 17 of 23 distractor cases (74%) return a declared distractor within top-5. At k=1 the same rate is 0%, which is the number a reader should treat as the retrieval hard-failure rate.
